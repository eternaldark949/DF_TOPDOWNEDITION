# Game data

Dimensions Freelancer's content, in the shapes the Directory defines: the prop catalog, map layouts, `GAME_DB`, character builds, items, weapons, vehicles and balance numbers. Data only, no behaviour. Game boot registers these libraries into the Directory's registry, and the single-file build packs them into `DFAB V8.html`.

`game-assets/generated/sdc-city.json` is the city as its seeded classic generator made it, exported once by `node tools/export-city.mjs` (`--check` fails when the generator no longer makes the same file): roads and their streets as the traffic network lays them out, blocks, pavements, crossings, floor zones, buildings (each with its `look` as the generator resolved it: sections, colours, height, the street it fronts and its entrance, sign, roof and facade features, street variant), walls, lamps, neon, foliage, decor and doors. Generated, so it is not edited by hand. `game-lighting.json`, `city-ground.json` and `city-buildings.json` are the looks of the native light layer, the city's ground and its street buildings (the eye height the walls lean from, floor height, and the shop, apartment and arcade kits).
