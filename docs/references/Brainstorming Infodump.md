#### Safe Houses

A safe house is a location where the player and their team can reside during downtime and return to when in danger or need of restocking supplies. Safe houses can be customized and I'm thinking about allowing players and/or team members to employ delivery services to the house within the game world. These safe spots can also be chosen as a respawn point when returning from the ethereal plane.

Players can play mini games with their team and engage in Bonding Questlines to improve their relationship with their team. In multiplayer, fellow players can become visitors and join in activities as well.

#### Multiplayer

##### Parallel Realities

This is a term used to represent the respective worlds of each player that sets foot in Dimensia. In its essence, each player has their own independent experience of the world (single player), which represents their reality while concurrently, other players live in parallel realities to them. During multiplayer, these parallel realities converge to allow them to meet each other and interact.

Certain byproducts can be found in the convergence of worlds, such as player Imprints, shared progression, puzzles requiring cooperation and tangible effects from the convergence of other players on their world.

##### Competitive/Cooperative Modes

Multiplayer will have two primary modes: competitive and cooperative. Players will have the choice to either clash or aid one another when their worlds converge.

Listed below are some ideas for competitive multiplayer activities:

###### Amusement Parks

Amusement Parks are a feature within the world of Dimensia, where players can compete to earn high scores and win special equipment, emotes and combat moves, otherwise unavailable in the main campaign.
###### Arena Battles

These could involve, free-for-alls, deathmatches, point captures and other similar modes where player compete against one another to gain the most points.
###### Scavenger Hunts

This sounds pretty straightforward. A commodity could be set and players have to collect as many as they can of it before time runs out. Whoever collects the most wins. On the other hand, there could be a set score to win and whoever reaches it first is the winner.
###### Timed Events

These could include any sort of competition where players must achieve an objective before the timer runs out. Survival activities are a great example of this.

These activities can me subtly modified to involve teamwork. Below, I will explain how in further detail.

###### Amusement Parks (Coop)

Amusement Park activities can be modified in cooperative mode to require players to work as a unit to complete games and activities, resulting in shared rewards.

###### Arena Battles (Coop)

Instead of fighting each other, players in this mode can join forces to defeat interdimensional glitches and creature invasions. They can also join forces to kill bosses and help each other overcome difficult obstacles.

###### Scavenger Hunts (Coop)

#### Dimensional Interaction/Lore

Dimensia is a world of infinite dimensions that exist within the same space. Dimensions exist within realms and realms are facets of a dimension "binder" if you will. A dimension "binder" is basically a complete collection of every dimension in existence. So yes, a dimension "binder" is infinite. Realms basically take each binder and *render* it in one facet. One dimension can have many realms. Let me break this down further:

Southern Dimensions City, aka SDC is a city residing within the world of Dimensia. SDC has the widely accepted "real" or official realm, however, there are other realms where it's in ruins, inhabited by vampiric creatures, floating in the sky and so on so forth.

##### Pocket Dimensions

Pocket dimensions are an interesting phenomenon in the world of *Dimensia*. They are an anomaly that can be used for many convenient purposes. For example, they can be used in safe houses to contain extra rooms that are not visible from the outside. In this manner, a small house can actually be much bigger than it appears.

The origin of pocket dimensions is still largely unknown beyond the fact that these rifts are rare and enigmatic, being extremely useful yet not entirely understood. Some believe that they are glitches in the fabric of the interdimensional realms.

##### Flitting

One of the core mechanics of the game and a core feature in the world of Dimensia is *flitting*, which is essentially the process of instantaneously transferring between Dimensions. This involves alternating the vibrational level of the *flitter's* body and aligning the frequency with that of the target dimension they wish to travel to. This kind of process grants the *flitter* a resilience of sorts to the basic elements of the dimension as essentially they're a part of this new reality. This does not mean that the *flitter* will necessarily survive attacks and other dangers in that dimension, but they will not get sick from base elements even if they are alien in nature.

Flitting has so many possible ways to implement it into the game and world. Let me share why:

Flitting has the potential to be used for not only travel, but combat (think dodging, stealth, hiding equipment, ambushes, etc.) Players can also form their own mastery of flitting, leading to some innovative uses of the abilities. Let's dive deeper into the various ways that *flitting* can be utilized.

###### Evasion/Dodging

Considering the fact that *flitting* involves spontaneously jumping between dimensions, it's only natural that it can be used as an evasion tactic as well. If done right, players can flit to another dimension just as an attack is landing and return to deliver devastating counter attacks. Also, using a temporary form of *Imprints*, like how bright lights remain on the retina for a bit even after closing your eyes, players can memorize the positions of opponents, flit and reappear behind them to gain the upper hand or escape combat entirely. It's easy to see how *flitting* in this manner can lead to takedowns and critical hits. Of course, flitting takes energy and as such, there is limit to how many times a player can do this in quick succession, leading to opportunities for more intentional uses of the ability.
*Flitting* can also be used to avoid detection when using stealth whether by enemies or security systems. In this way, players can also navigate puzzles or challenging terrain, such as broken bridges with strategic placement.

###### Equipment Concealment

The discovery of pocket dimensions and multilayered dimensional presence has led to other innovative uses of *flitting*. Players can use this to hide equipped weapons or morph the appearance of the weapons to give enemies a false idea of their true equipment. Tying into the energy cost of using this, players would only be able to keep this up for a limited time and would have a cooldown period before being able to use it again.
In combat, this can easily translate into barraging enemies from unexpected angles and directions with unseen weapons and even evolving to switching loadouts on the fly as the player becomes more skilled.

###### Flit Maps/Safe Zones

*Flitting* can come with risks such as *flitting* into objects, or hazardous zones and as such, there are flit maps that have been designed including safe zones that are guaranteed not to put the *flitter* at risk. Here introduces a new character stat called Flit Attunement that increases the chances that the player will not flit into a dangerous zone or phase into an object.

#### Combat

Combat is a big part of Dimensions as a game and there are quite a few details that need to be covered here.

##### Improvised Weapons

Players can take non-weapons, like cups, bottles, sticks, instruments, ladders, etc. and use them as weapons on the fly during combat encounters. This encourages spatial awareness and nuanced strategic use of the environment.

##### Prone Combat

Being knocked on the floor doesn't necessarily have to end combat. You can grapple if on the ground, punch, kick and whatnot or stomp opponents who are down while you're still up. There is also prone dodging such as rolling left and right to avoid attacks, as well as prone blocking and parrying that can provide opportunities to get back on their feet. *Flitting* has a high potential to be applied here as well.

##### Realtime Hand Switching

Players can switch equipped items between hands in realtime, without using menus, facilitating continuous engagement with combat situations or even exploration.

##### Team Aid

Imagine that you ran out of magazines for your gun, but you can call out to teammates for a spare mag and they can throw it over to you in the midst of combat. There can be a mechanic that if the player presses the reload button just as they catch it, they get a quick reload. This can also apply to needing spare weapons or sidearms.

##### Dance

Dance can be incorporated into combat for extra agility and style in combat encounters. Some bosses, such as the Four Men have dance choreography incorporated into their moveset, given their history of being performers.

##### Melee Details

Melee combat has some deeper concepts ingrained into it to prevent it from being surface level beyond even prone combat. There are blocks, parries, grabs, and more to choose from.

#### Traversal

Traversal, like combat, is another integral part of the world of Dimensions. Through a blend of *flitting* and *parkour*, players can interact with the environment around them in dynamic, fluid, and engaging ways.

##### Parkour

Parkour is a big part of movement, utilizing momentum, elevation and stylish moves to allow players to become a harnesser of their environment. There are potentials to make mistakes as well, however, encouraging players to have deep spatial awareness. A pretty good example is leaping from pole to pole and hitting a tree in the middle. Fall distance and momentum can also impact the environment. For example, jumping from a certain critical height onto a glass ceiling window or a rotting wooden beam can cause them to break and the player to fall through. Sprinting into a glass window, can cause it to shatter and so on so forth. Parkour can also see integration into combat. Vaulting over something and into someone can stun someone, wall running and jumping towards opponents with a punch can drastically increase impact damage. Jumping off a beam and onto someone can knock them out. You get the idea.

Another aspect of parkour is a feature called the Flow State, where successive jumps and maneuver gradually boosts the player's speed. However, this will come at a higher risk of messing up, such as difficulty slowing down, overshooting or toppling off of thin or precarious objects/ledges upon landing. The more the player masters the parkour the more that they can do.

##### Driving

Driving is another aspect of traversal that can significantly impact how players interact with the world. Vehicles are not only a means of transportation, they are a means of expression and a useful extension of combat and magic. Most vehicles within Dimensia, are integrated with a built-in AI system that allows them to have extended functionality such as autodrive, autoaim (for built-in weapons), and conversational interactions. Most vehicles within Dimensions are designed to be fast and can be infused with *flitting* capabilities.
Some basic mechanics for driving include, the ability to floor the accelerator and get out of a car without turning it off.

#### Life/Death

*Dimensia* has two planes of existence: the material and the ethereal. When characters are killed or the player dies, they are transported to the ethereal plane, where they assume an incorporeal, spirit-esque form.
Here, a rare resource called Resurrection Crystals can be used to reverse death and rejoin a spirit with its body. The material and ethereal planes have their own stories and questlines, ensuring that players don't find themselves with nothing to do and entering into tedium. Even the creatures and encounters there will be unique to their respective planes with minimal overlap. To add intrigue there will be unique loot, rewards, and progression in the latter, including spirit related upgrades.
Mechanics will be slightly different in the ethereal plane as well. For example, players can phase through structures and obstacles, fly, possess objects or creatures, and wield spirit energy using their Spirit Mass.

##### Respawning

To mitigate the overreliance on Resurrection Crystals, there will be a timer that will delay the player coming back to life until their medic and upgrades specialist, Dr. Yin, finishes repairing their body. Also each revival by Dr. Yin, grants 2 biostimulators that can be used to revive in the heat of battle. These can either be used when dying in the material plane or saved for more useful situations. Also, teammates can revive the player if they arrive in time. In the case that the player's body was vaporized, when the timer runs out the Material plane's matrix will generate a new body for them to inhabit of its own accord.
Also, in key areas in the ethereal plane, there will be Healing Shrines and their rarer variant, the Resurgence Altar, where players can collect Ethereal Resurgence Points. These points can be used to bypass the wait time as well. Another idea in the works is to have random invasions of powerful spirits who grant lucrative rewards such as a lot of Ethereal Resurgence Points and possibly even Resurrection Crystals.

Spots where players die will also become Imprints in the world.

##### Ethereal "Death"

Since spirits cannot die, health reducing to zero in the Ethereal plane can create a state of Spiritual Exhaustion, cresting an effect called Ephemeral Dissipation, sending them to the nearest Healing Shrine or Resurgence Altar. Also, deaths as a result of evil spirits or dark energies can result in a gradual Ethereal Corruption that weakens the spirit's resilience and purity in exchange for raw strength and higher damage output.
To return to their pure state, players can enter into Spiritual Trials where they must face their demons in order to break free from corruption.

##### Unfinished Business

Stay tuned for this idea in progress, a concept where spirits returning to the Ethereal Plane continue their last ethereal questline in a mode called, you guessed it: Unfinished Business.

##### Back In the Game

The official phrase for a spirit finally returning to the material plane.

#### Resources

There are a plethora of resources within Dimensia that can be scavenged or utilized. In this section, I will describe them in further detail.

##### Dark Element

The lifeblood of the corporation's power base, Dark Element is the single rock that is keeping the plutocracy afloat. Harnessed for its near infinite power generation capability, it is the reason that massive pieces of technology such as the interdimensional UNet WiFi transmitter, and planet sized ships can exist. With power like this and the highly stable nature of Dark Element, energy is cost efficient and very safe to run. However, access to the resource is gated off by the corporations in what is known as the Dark Element Mine. When harnessed from the deep black rock, its energy appears like neon purple light.
